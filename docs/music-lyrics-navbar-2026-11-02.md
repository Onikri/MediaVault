# Lyrics identity and navbar music

The upload caption is no longer the catalogue query. `music-identity.js` separates
the supplied artist aliases (including native script and Latin names), removes
repeated artist prefixes/suffixes and service labels, and preserves version
qualifiers. The resulting identity is used by lyrics, music search and metadata.
No transliteration, lyric text or word timing is generated by a model.

LRCLIB first receives a structured exact request. An exact miss falls back to
parallel structured searches across at most two supplied title forms and three
artist aliases, under one 12-second cancellation boundary. Candidates must match
the title, an artist alias and version, and be within 2.5 seconds of the actual
audio duration. Album identity improves ranking when supplied. Covers, live
versions, reversed artist/title metadata and short clips do not become matches
just because they are the first result. Plain text remains readable in the panel
without fabricated synchronization; timed TTML can upgrade line/plain results.

Live verification on 2026-11-02: the mixed caption
`米津玄師 Kenshi Yonezu — IRIS OUT Kenshi Yonezu (米津玄師)` resolved through
LRCLIB to record **33970958**, artist **Kenshi Yonezu**, duration **153 s**,
with line timings. This verifies lookup, not the availability of every song or
the accuracy of community timings. Lyrics are not copied into this repository.
Musixmatch was not added as an anonymous provider: its official SDK/API requires
an application API key ([official SDK](https://github.com/musixmatch/musixmatch-sdk)).
No such credentials were supplied for this integration.

The full navbar music strip expands left while retaining its right edge. Its
38 px height and cover-backed Play circle match the logo/search capsule. Music
details share the search panel's glass material and surface-only spring; their
edges follow the full strip. Search has priority over expanded music and uses a
deterministic target width rather than measuring an animated intermediate width.
Navbar groups move through position transforms; scrolled outer edges follow the
same responsive container as the page. Brand text and Search use the exact
primary-button accent. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Validation includes Node identity/ranking/cancellation/plain-text contracts,
Python music metadata/audio contracts, and Chromium UI checks at mobile/desktop
sizes. Provider responses in browser tests are fixtures; HLS/audio bytes are
actually decoded, and TTML word progress uses supplied fixture timestamps. The
live LRCLIB check above is separate. No new ML inference is part of this change.
The frontend deployment does not require a VM restart or server key changes.


## Follow-up: fixed transport and title-section handoff

The hovered mini transport is now the size reference: **344 × 38 px**. The lyric
strip and its details retain that width and right edge; they do not cover the
profile button. Free space on the strip toggles its mode, while playback buttons
and range inputs keep their own actions. The muted accent fill shows the actual
elapsed fraction across the whole strip. A captured horizontal Play drag maps
absolute cursor position to time, independently of the previous song position;
vertical drag and wheel still adjust volume, shown by the ring. The time preview
uses 11 px tabular text. Icon hover targets are square circles, and expand/collapse
arrows switch with a spring. All music accents reference the same theme variable
as Search and primary collection actions.

Closed Search is always a 38 px circle, including after closing/reopening and
scroll changes. Navbar dimensions no longer have conflicting collection-specific
rules. Desktop capsule left/top/bottom insets are 10 px (9 px on phones).

`TitleNavigation` owns one extensible section registry. A matching-width capsule
aligns under the brand; crossing the header transfers it with a brief stretched
capsule animation into the brand position. There is no second sticky band. The
header label follows the current section, including the last section at the
bottom of the document, and opens a keyboard-operable section menu. Reverse
scroll restores the brand. `extraSections` lets integrations add a section without
adding another navigation implementation.

Lyric search also separates bilingual title aliases such as `Otonoke (オトノケ)`.
It tries keyword indexing as well as structured fields, with at most two requests
in flight and a total budget of two retries respecting HTTP Retry-After (bounded
by the shared 12-second timeout). Duplicate normalized metadata no longer
restarts a lookup. The provider returned real `503 ServerOverloaded` replies
during this investigation; these are reported as temporary errors. A matching
song within a small video-padding tolerance can show plain text without applying
incorrect timings. Wrong artists, covers, substantially different durations and
versions remain rejected.
